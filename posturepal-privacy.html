PosturePal Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026  |  App: PosturePal (com.eversnap.posturepal)  |  Contact: posturepal@eversnap.com

1. Overview

PosturePal is a posture correction and digital wellness app for Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. All user data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers or collect personal data.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

PosturePal does not collect, transmit, or store on any server: your name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or accelerometer readings. Sensor data is processed entirely on-device and never leaves your phone.

3. Local Storage

The following data is stored only on your device using SharedPreferences and SQLite (Drift ORM): posture calibration baseline, daily check-in streak, Dew Drops balance, app settings, and meditation pot state. This data never leaves your device unless you make an in-app purchase.

4. Advertising

Free users see rewarded interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device Advertising ID for ad targeting and measurement. EU users are shown a consent dialog before personalised ads are displayed. You can opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings > Google > Ads. Premium subscribers see no ads.

5. Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed via Google Play Billing and managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase token and subscription status only. Payment details are handled entirely by Google and are never seen by PosturePal.

6. Third-Party SDKs

• Google AdMob — ad serving (free tier): Privacy Policy
• RevenueCat — subscription management: Privacy Policy
• Google Play Services — in-app billing: Privacy Policy

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all PosturePal data from your device at any time: open PosturePal > Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete all my data. Deletion is immediate. RevenueCat subscription records are retained per their payment compliance requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

PosturePal is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

9. GDPR (EU Users)

EU residents have the right to access and delete their data using the in-app delete button. For data inquiries please contact: posturepal@eversnap.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The Last Updated date at the top of this page reflects any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact
